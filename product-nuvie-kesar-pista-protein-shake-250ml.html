About this product

The brand likens the taste to kesar pista kulfi. It is made with ultra filtered toned milk and a little cream.

It is heat treated in the sealed bottle (retort processing), so it keeps without a fridge until you open it.

Good to know

How to enjoy
Best served chilled. Shake before opening and drink within 24 hours of opening.
