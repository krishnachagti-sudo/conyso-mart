Why we exist

The new Indian food brands people see online, the protein bars, makhana, dried fruit and fizzy drinks made by young companies, rarely reach a town like Paonta Sahib. Big distributors wait for sales history that a new brand cannot have yet, so the shelves here stay the same. Conyso Mart is a distributor that takes those brands on.

  1. New Indian brands
    Young companies, mostly sold online and in big cities.
  2. Conyso Mart, Paonta SahibWe sign them, keep stock here, and check every label.
  3. YouAt your door, free and the same day. Soon on the shelves of local shops too.

What we do

  • DistributeWe sign the brands and are starting to supply shops in the town, so you can buy them offline.
  • DeliverOrder online and we bring it to your door free, the same day if you order by 6 pm. Pay in cash or by UPI when it arrives.
  • Tell peopleWe show what is new on Instagram, so people in town know these brands exist.
  • Check the factsEvery product page shows what the pack label says, and every brand fact has a source.
