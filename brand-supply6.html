The Supply6 story

Supply6 is a Bengaluru supplements brand founded in 2020 by Vaibhav Bhandari and Rahul Jacob. Its flagship product is Supply6 360, a daily sachet that the company says combines more than 63 nutrients with probiotics and plant ingredients.

It has also launched Supply6 Salts, a zero-sugar hydration mix. The company sells through its own website, Amazon and Flipkart, and quick-commerce apps such as Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart.

In 2026 Supply6 raised ₹48 crore in a round led by Unilever Ventures, with Zeropearl VC and actor Kriti Sanon also investing. At the time it reported an annualised revenue run-rate of ₹75 crore and said most of its revenue came from two products.
