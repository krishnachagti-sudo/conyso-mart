Most distributors in small towns turn down new brands: no sales history, unknown rotation, expiry risk. We take them on.

What we do for a brand

  1. Shops. We are starting to supply local shops in Paonta Sahib: kiranas, supermarkets, gyms and cafes.
  2. Doorstep. We deliver to shoppers free, the same day, from our own stock.
  3. Demand. We post about the brand on Instagram locally, so people go looking for it.
  4. Later. A store of our own that carries only new brands.
