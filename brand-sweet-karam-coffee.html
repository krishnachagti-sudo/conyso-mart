The Sweet Karam Coffee story

Sweet Karam Coffee is a Chennai food brand founded in 2015 by Anand Bharadwaj, Nalini Parthiban, Srivatsan Sundararaman and Veera Raghavan. It sells South Indian sweets and snacks, filter coffee, condiments and ghee.

The company sells through its own website, online marketplaces and quick-commerce apps, and says it serves customers in 32 countries.

In 2025 it raised an $8 million Series A led by Peak XV Partners, about two years after a $1.5 million round from Fireside Ventures. Inc42 reported that its operating revenue rose to INR 11.2 crore in FY24, while its loss also grew.
